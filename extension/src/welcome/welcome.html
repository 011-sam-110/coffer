Coffer

Coffer is in this browser

Coffer fills and saves your logins from the Coffer app on this PC. Your passwords stay in Coffer's vault on this PC.

Connect it to Coffer

  1. Pin the Coffer button: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin next to Coffer.
  2. Click the Coffer button, then Connect.
  3. Coffer shows four words. Check that the Coffer button shows the same words, then click Allow in Coffer.

Your browser's own password offers

This browser also offers to save passwords. Turn its offers off, so you see one offer, from Coffer.
